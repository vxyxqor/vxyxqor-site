## What HeyGen Gets Right

### 1. Avatar realism is in a different league

HeyGen's avatars blink, micro-express, and gesture in a way that feels human. The difference is subtle but critical: Synthesia avatars look polished but sterile. HeyGen avatars look like they just walked out of a Zoom call. For sales videos where trust matters, this is the deciding factor.

### 2. Voice cloning from 30 seconds of audio

We recorded 30 seconds of a team member's voice. HeyGen cloned it in under 3 minutes. The cloned voice speaking Spanish and Mandarin was not perfect — there was a slight accent drift — but it was recognizable and natural enough for internal training. For English, the clone was indistinguishable from the real thing.

### 3. 40+ languages with proper lip sync

We generated the same script in English, Spanish, German, Japanese, Hindi, French, Portuguese, and Arabic. The lip sync was accurate in all eight. Arabic and Japanese were particularly impressive because these languages have phonemes that break most Western-built lip-sync engines.

### 4. API for bulk generation

We stress-tested the API by generating 100 personalized sales outreach videos ("Hi {first_name}, I noticed {company} is hiring..."). Total time: 47 minutes. Cost: $29 in credits. That is $0.29 per personalized video. A traditional video agency would charge $500–$2,000 for the same output.

## Where HeyGen Falls Short

### 1. Avatar gestures can feel repetitive

After watching 20+ videos, you start noticing the same hand movements. HeyGen has about 12 gesture templates per avatar. For a small batch, it is fine. For a 50-video course, you will want to mix avatars or accept some repetition.

### 2. Commercial rights require higher tiers

The Creator plan at $29/month does not include full commercial rights for client work. You need the Business plan at $89/month for that. If you are an agency creating videos for clients, factor this into your pricing.

### 3. Custom avatar creation takes time

Uploading your own photo to create a custom avatar requires 5–10 minutes of video footage and a 24-hour processing window. The result is good, but not as polished as HeyGen's stock avatars. For now, we recommend using stock avatars for client work and custom avatars for internal content.
